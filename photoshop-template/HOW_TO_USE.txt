FRAMED CANVAS SMART OBJECT TEMPLATE

Fastest workflow

1. Open Framed_Canvas_Smart_Object_Template.psdt in Photoshop.
   A .psdt opens as a fresh untitled copy, so the master stays clean.
2. In the Layers panel, double-click the thumbnail on:
      YOUR ARTWORK - DOUBLE-CLICK ME
3. In the PSB tab that opens, choose File > Place Embedded and select your artwork.
4. Press Enter/Return to confirm placement. If needed, use Ctrl+T so the artwork covers the full PSB canvas.
5. Press Ctrl+S, then close the PSB tab. Your artwork will update inside the frame automatically.
6. Export the finished mockup as JPG or PNG.

Keep these layers turned on:

- FRAME + INNER SHADOW - KEEP ON
- CANVAS TEXTURE - KEEP ON
- CANVAS LIGHTING - KEEP ON

The Smart Object is sized and positioned to the photographed canvas opening. Artwork extends slightly beneath the frame edge to prevent white seams.

Tip: artwork close to a 4:5 portrait ratio will require the least cropping.
