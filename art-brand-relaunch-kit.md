Your current Etsy listings already point toward a clear lane: motivational wall art for people chasing a better life. The strongest version of this brand is not "random canvas prints." It is a shop for ambition, discipline, money mindset, and future-focused rooms.

Recommended positioning:

**Motivational wall art for builders, dreamers, and people becoming harder to stop.**

Primary customer:
- Entrepreneurs and side-hustlers
- Students and graduates
- Gym and self-improvement people
- Creators, barbers, gamers, streamers, and home-office buyers
- People decorating bedrooms, offices, studios, dorms, and apartments

Product focus:
- Canvas prints
- Framed posters
- Unframed posters
- Digital downloads later
- Limited drops later

## Pricing Note

Your screenshot shows prices around $55-$210. That can work for canvas, but you should make sure your print-on-demand cost, Etsy fees, shipping, and ads still leave margin.

Starter structure:

- Posters: lower entry price
- Canvas prints: main product
- Framed prints: premium option
- Limited designs: occasional higher price

## Payment Setup

For now, keep checkout on Etsy because Etsy handles buyer payment options through Etsy Payments in eligible markets. You connect your bank account in Etsy, and Etsy deposits your available funds.

Later, when you build a standalone store:

1. Start with Shopify if you want a full store.
2. Use Stripe if you want a custom website checkout.
3. Keep Etsy as a marketplace channel even after launching your own site.

## Print-On-Demand Recommendation

Start with **Printful + Etsy**.

Why:
- Easier setup
- Good product mockups
- Canvas and poster options
- Cleaner beginner workflow

Also compare Printify before committing because it may offer better pricing on some products.

## 30-Day Relaunch Plan

Week 1:
- Choose the shop name
- Update Etsy banner, icon, announcement, and about section
- Pick 12 strongest listings to keep active
- Rewrite titles and tags for those 12

Week 2:
- Connect Printful or Printify
- Replace weak mockups with consistent product mockups
- Create 3 shop sections
- Add 2-3 size options per product

Week 3:
- Launch the website landing page
- Link website to Etsy
- Add email signup later with Mailchimp, Shopify, or ConvertKit
- Make 5 short videos showing room mockups and product closeups

Week 4:
- Add 5 new designs in one collection
- Test Etsy ads with a small daily budget only on strongest listings
- Track favorites, clicks, conversion rate, and search terms
